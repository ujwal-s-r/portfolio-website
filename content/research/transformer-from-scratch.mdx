## Motivation & Architecture

Implemented the complete GPT-style Decoder-Only Transformer architecture from raw mathematical foundations in C++ and CUDA.

### Components Built From First Principles

- **Manual Reverse-Mode Autograd**: Built a lightweight computation graph engine supporting dynamic tensor backprop.
- **Custom SGEMM & Tiled MatMul**: Wrote 2D block-tiled matrix multiplication kernels achieving ~85% of cuBLAS theoretical peak FLOPS.
- **RoPE (Rotary Position Embedding)**: Vectorized SIMD complex rotation kernel for length extrapolation.
- **BPE Tokenizer**: Byte-Pair Encoding tokenizer written in pure C++ with trie lookup.
